LIDAR VR

LIDAR VR is an interactive educational Virtual Reality experience I developed to teach users about the applications of Light Detection and Ranging (LiDAR) technology in forestry. Designed with accessibility and engagement in mind, the game transports users into a virtual forest where they can visualize LiDAR data in 3D and interact with real-world scenarios that showcase how this technology is used to monitor ecosystems, analyze forest density, and promote sustainable land management.

    Working closely with faculty from the Biology and Environmental Science departments, I:
  • Designed game mechanics and user interactions tailored for education.
  • Programmed interactive elements using Unity and C#.
  • Implemented VR functionality for Meta Quest and Quest 2 using OpenXR and Oculus SDK.
  • Created intuitive UX, ensuring players of varying ages could navigate and understand the environment.
